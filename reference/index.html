    Software used in the workflow via Galaxy

    fastp

    Chen, S., et al. (2018). "fastp: an ultra-fast all-in-one FASTQ preprocessor." Bioinformatics 34(17): i884-i890. https://github.com/OpenGene/fastp.

    MultiQC

    Ewels, P., et al. (2016). "MultiQC: summarize analysis results for multiple tools and samples in a single report." Bioinformatics 32(19): 3047-3048. http://multiqc.infoContact:  phil.ewels@scilifelab.se

    Kraken2

    Wood, D. E., et al. (2019). "Improved metagenomic analysis with Kraken 2." Genome Biology 20(1): 257. https://ccb.jhu.edu/software/kraken2/

    Mycobacterium specific kraken2 database

    Hall, M. B. (2023). Mycobacterium representative kraken2 database. Zenodo. https://zenodo.org/records/8339822

    Krakentools

    Lu, J., et al. (2022). "Metagenome analysis using the Kraken software suite." Nature Protocols 17(12): 2815-2839. https://ccb.jhu.edu/software/krakentools/

    NCBI Taxonomy database

    Schoch, C. L., et al. (2020). "NCBI Taxonomy: a comprehensive update on curation, resources and tools." Database: The Journal of Biological Databases and Curation 2020. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/taxonomy.

    Snippy

    Seemann, T. (2020). "Snippy: rapid haploid variant calling and core genome alignment." GitHub https://github. com/tseemann/snippy. Accessed 10. https://github.com/tseemann/snippy

    BWA

    Li, H. and R. Durbin (2009). "Fast and accurate short read alignment with Burrows-Wheeler transform." Bioinformatics 25(14): 1754-1760. http://maq.sourceforge.net.

    FreeBayes

    Garrison, E. and G. Marth (2012). "Haplotype-based variant detection from short-read sequencing." arXiv preprint arXiv:1207.3907. https://github.com/freebayes/freebayes

    TB Variant Filter

    Heusden, P. v. (2021). TB Variant Filter. https://github.com/COMBAT-TB/tb_variant_filter

    SnpEff

    Cingolani, P., et al. (2012). "A program for annotating and predicting the effects of single nucleotide polymorphisms, SnpEff." Fly 6(2): 80-92. https://pcingola.github.io/SnpEff/

    TB-Profiler

    Coll, F., et al. (2015). "Rapid determination of anti-tuberculosis drug resistance from whole-genome sequences." Genome Medicine 7(1): 51. https://tbdr.lshtm.ac.uk

    TB-Profiler database

    Phelan, J., et al. (2024). Standard database for the TBProfiler tool, GitHub. https://github.com/jodyphelan/tbdb

    TB Variant Report

    COMBAT-TB (2019). tbvcfreport. GitHub repository, GitHub. https://github.com/COMBAT-TB/tbvcfreport

    JBrowse

    Skinner, M. E., et al. (2009). "JBrowse: a next-generation genome browser." Genome Research 19(9): 1630-1638. https://jbrowse.org/jb2/

    SAMtools

    Li, H., et al. (2009). "The Sequence Alignment/Map format and SAMtools." Bioinformatics 25(16): 2078-2079. http://samtools.sourceforge.net.

    Find SNP sites

    Page, A. J., et al. (2016). "SNP-sites: rapid efficient extraction of SNPs from multi-FASTA alignments." Microb Genom 2(4): e000056.https://sanger-pathogens.github.io/snp-sites/

    SNP distance matrix

    Seemann, T., et al. (2021). "Pairwise SNP distance matrix from a FASTA sequence alignment. https://github.com/tseemann/snp-dists.

    Scipy

    Virtanen, P., et al. (2020). "SciPy 1.0: fundamental algorithms for scientific computing in Python." Nature Methods 17(3): 261-272. https://scipy.org/

    IQ-Tree

    Minh, B. Q., et al. (2020). "IQ-TREE 2: New Models and Efficient Methods for Phylogenetic Inference in the Genomic Era." Molecular Biology and Evolution 37(5): 1530-1534.http://www.iqtree.org,

    iTOL

    Letunic, I. and P. Bork (2024). "Interactive Tree of Life (iTOL) v6: recent updates to the phylogenetic tree display and annotation tool." Nucleic Acids Research 52(W1): W78-W82. https://itol.embl.de/
